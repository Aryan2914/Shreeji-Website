Yes! We offer on-site diagnosis and repair for homes and offices in Ahmedabad. For major repairs, we pick up your device safely and deliver it back once resolved.

Absolutely. All new laptops, printers, and CCTV equipment come with the official brand warranty. For our repair services, we provide a standard 3-month service warranty.
